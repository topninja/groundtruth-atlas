What is sourced

Zillow ZHVI and ZORI, HUD Fair Market Rents, Census property-tax data, Treasury FIO/NAIC premium and loss metrics, Senate non-renewal data, and FEMA disaster declarations.

Sourced figures are transformed only for aggregation or ratios and are labeled throughout.

What is modeled

2023–2025 premium estimates use national approved-rate anchors. Effective cap rate applies a 23% operating stack. The Repricing Index blends premium growth, non-renewal, loss ratio, and recent declarations.

These are screening tools, not quotes, appraisals, or investment advice. Read the methodology and caveats.
