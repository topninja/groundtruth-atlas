Deal calculator — insurance-adjusted underwrite

What this instrument shows

Investors still underwrite single-family deals with 2021-era insurance assumptions. CAP//RATE re-prices every county in the FIO dataset: the same house, the same rent, the same expense stack — first with its 2021 homeowners premium, then with its current one. The difference is the insurance drag, in basis points of cap rate.

In most of the Midwest it is noise. On the Gulf, it is the deal. Companion to The Premium Map, which supplies the county premium series.

Method, short form

Effective cap rate = (12 × rent − 23% operating stack MODELED − county property tax at the ACS effective rate SOURCED − homeowners premium) ÷ ZHVI value.

gross = 12 × rent
EGI = gross × (1 − 0.23)
tax = ACS rate × value
NOI = EGI − tax − premium
cap = NOI ÷ value
drag = (p₂₅ − p₂₁) ÷ value × 10,000 bps

Caveats that matter

ZHVI is a mid-tier owner index, not investor purchase prices; ZORI is asking rent; FMR is a 40th-percentile rent and understates market in the 1,650 counties where it is the fallback. FIO county premiums are composition-smoothed ZIP means.

Cap-rate levels are modeled estimates. The cross-county differences and the insurance drag are the point of the instrument. Full sources, caveats, and reproduction steps live in the README.
